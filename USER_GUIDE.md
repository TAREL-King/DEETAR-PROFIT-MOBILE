# DEETAR PROFIT Mobile Edition - Customer Guide

**Know Your Numbers. Grow Your Business.**

## 1. Calculate a product's profit

Open **Calculator**. Enter the quantity produced or purchased and the quantity sold, then enter the product price and costs. Yellow fields are editable. Results update as you type and save in this browser.

- Enter costs per unit for product, packaging, delivery support, advertising and other variable expenses.
- Enter discounts and payment-processing fees as percentages, such as `5` for 5%.
- Enter fixed expenses for the batch once. The calculator subtracts those fixed expenses once from total batch profit.
- Payment fees are calculated on the discounted selling price.
- Product cost for unsold units appears separately. Profit only counts product cost for units sold, so unsold stock is not charged twice.
- A warning appears when quantity sold is higher than quantity produced or purchased.
- The suggested list price reaches the target profit margin after fixed expenses are allocated across units sold. Break-even price uses the same fixed-expense allocation.

## 2. Record and manage sales

Open **Sales** and complete the date, product, quantity, price and cost fields. Choose **Save sale**. Revenue, expenses and profit calculate automatically. Each record can be edited or deleted. You can export all recorded rows to CSV for a spreadsheet.

Use **Fixed expense assigned to this sale** to include an appropriate share of business overhead in the record. Do not enter the same overhead again in another sale.

## 3. Review your dashboard

The dashboard totals the saved records. It shows total sales, revenue, expenses, estimated profit and profit margin. The product insight ranks products by recorded profit. Monthly performance shows the latest six calendar months.

## 4. Change currency display

Choose NGN, USD, GBP, EUR, GHS or CAD from the currency menu. This changes the displayed currency symbol and code only. It never converts amounts. Keep all records in one currency. If you switch the display, existing numbers keep their original numeric values and will be shown using the newly selected currency.

## 5. Save, back up and move your data

Calculator inputs and sales records stay in this browser on this device. They are not uploaded to DEETAR PROFIT. To keep a backup, open **Guide > Download backup** and save the JSON file somewhere safe. On another device, open the app and choose **Restore backup**. Restoring replaces the app data currently stored in that browser.

Use **Download this calculation** for a text copy of the current product estimate. The dashboard can also be downloaded as a summary.

## 6. Add the app to your phone

Open the shared HTTPS link in your mobile browser.

- **Android:** use the browser menu and choose **Install app** or **Add to Home screen** when available.
- **iPhone or iPad:** open the link in Safari, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.

The browser may use different wording depending on device and version. After the app has loaded once, its service worker can cache the app files for offline use. Keep backups even when offline access is enabled.

## Important limits

This tool provides estimates from the information entered. It does not provide tax or accounting advice, foreign-exchange conversion, bank connections, inventory tracking, payment processing, or automatic order import. The sales tracker and dashboard are local to the current browser and do not sync.
