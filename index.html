How these results are calculated

Net price = list price less discount. Payment fees use the discounted price. Profit = net revenue less product cost for units sold, packaging, delivery support, advertising, other variable expenses, payment fees and batch fixed expenses. Unsold product cost is shown separately and is not counted as a sold-unit expense.

Break-even price and the suggested list price allocate batch fixed expenses across the quantity sold. Break-even quantity is the number of units needed to cover fixed expenses at the current discounted contribution per unit. The target margin is calculated after this fixed expense allocation.

A FRIENDLY START

Quick guide

Simple steps to use DEETAR PROFIT Mobile Edition.

01

Price a product

Enter your selling price, quantities and per-unit costs in Calculator. The suggested list price uses your target contribution margin before fixed expenses.

02

Record a sale

In Sales, enter the transaction details. The app calculates discounted revenue, payment fees, expenses and estimated profit. Use Edit or Delete to manage a record.

03

Review your business

Dashboard summarizes saved sales and compares revenue and profit across the last six calendar months. Best-performing product is ranked by recorded profit.

04

Export or move your data

Export sales as CSV for a spreadsheet. Download a JSON backup to move your saved data to another browser or device, then use Restore backup.

05

Your privacy and currency

Your calculator inputs and sales records are stored in this browser on this device. They are not uploaded to DEETAR PROFIT. Clearing browser storage can erase them, so keep a backup. Currency selection changes the display label and formatting only. It never converts amounts; do not mix currencies in one set of records.

Backup and restore

Download a backup file before changing device or clearing browser data.
